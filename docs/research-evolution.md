# Delivery Evolution

The engagement evolved through successive production milestones rather than a single static build.

## Phase 1: deterministic sector analytics

The earliest technical priority was to make company-level financial metrics and comparisons reproducible from the available sector data.

This established the principle that financial arithmetic and ranking behavior must remain deterministic and testable.

## Phase 2: broader analytical channels

The platform expanded from structured comparisons into free-text and evidence-grounded analytical modes.

Natural-language flexibility was added without moving numerical authority into the language model.

## Phase 3: multi-country comparability

A second country context increased the importance of explicit normalization, scope control and cross-country presentation rules.

Comparability had to be treated as an engineering problem rather than assumed from similar-looking spreadsheet columns.

## Phase 4: reporting and production controls

The delivery added structured exports, multilingual presentation, production runbooks, privacy checks and stronger release evidence.

## Phase 5: commercial and operational hardening

Later work introduced account/access controls, usage-credit/commercial workflows, administration paths and final production stabilization.

## Ongoing maintenance

The private system remains under controlled production maintenance. Public documentation intentionally avoids milestone-specific client acceptance details, exact ranking rules and private commercial configuration.
